**Last updated:** September 26, 2026
**Effective date:** September 26, 2026

This policy applies to WakilAI on iOS and Android.

WakilAI ("we," "us," "our") provides an AI-powered legal information assistant for
Uzbekistan. This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, who we
share it with, and the choices you have.

and we do not send them to Meta. The advertising identifiers and events in the two rows
above are used only to measure our own ads (see §5).

## 3. How we collect it

- Directly from you: account sign-in, typed/dictated questions, uploaded files, project
  configuration.
- Automatically: basic usage and diagnostic data as you use the app, and advertising
  identifiers and app events through the Meta SDK (see §5).
- From sign-in providers: Google, Apple, or Telegram share your name, email, and a
  provider ID when you authenticate, per your settings with that provider.

## 4. How we use your data

- To generate answers to your legal questions, including by sending your query,
  attached documents, and recent conversation history to AI service providers (see
  §5) that process this data to produce a response.
- To extract text from scanned documents and images you upload (OCR).
- To power search across documents within your projects.
- To remember preferences and context you've shared, so the assistant doesn't have to
  ask you to repeat yourself. This runs automatically as part of using the assistant;
  there is currently no separate toggle to turn it off.
- To transcribe voice questions into text.
- To retrieve current information from the web when your question requires it.
- To maintain your account, session history, and subscription status.
- To monitor and improve system reliability, including detecting app crashes.
- To measure our own advertising: which Instagram and Facebook ads lead to app installs,
  sign-ups, and purchases (see §5).
- To communicate with you about your account or the service.

We do not sell your personal data. Apart from the advertising measurement described in
§5 (Meta), we do not share it for third-party advertising.

### Advertising measurement (Meta)

We advertise WakilAI on Instagram and Facebook. To measure which ads lead to installs,
sign-ups, and purchases, we use Meta's SDK in the app and Meta's Conversions API on our
servers, and share the following with Meta Platforms:

- **From the app (Meta SDK):** app install and app-open events; a sign-up completed
  event with the sign-in method (Google, Apple, or Telegram); basic device and app
  details (such as app and OS version, device model, language, and time zone); your
  advertising ID, if your device allows it; Meta's app-specific anonymous ID; and your IP
  address, as with any internet connection.
- **From our servers (Conversions API):** when a payment is confirmed, a purchase event
  with the amount, currency, time, and order ID, your advertising ID, Meta's anonymous
  ID, and your platform, app version, and OS version.

We do not send your name, email address, phone number, questions, documents, voice
recordings, or conversation history to Meta. Unlike the providers in the table above,
Meta is not acting only as our processor: it handles this data under
[its own privacy policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/). Meta may match these
events to people who saw our ads on Instagram or Facebook, which Apple calls "tracking";
on iOS we ask for your permission first (see §8).

We do not share your data with data brokers.

## 7. Data retention

- Conversation history and account data are retained for as long as your account is
  active, so you can review past sessions.
- You can delete your account at any time in Settings. This immediately deactivates
  your account: you're signed out, can no longer log back in with that identity, and
  your conversation history, projects, and files are no longer accessible to you or
  visible to others. We are working toward fully automated erasure of this data across
  all our systems within a fixed window after deactivation; some data, including
  information used to personalize your experience across conversations, may take
  longer to be fully removed while this work is completed.
- The advertising identifiers and device details we store on your account (advertising
  ID, Meta's anonymous ID, platform, and app and OS versions) are removed when you delete
  your account. Data we have already sent to Meta cannot be recalled by us and is kept
  under Meta's privacy policy.

## 8. Your rights and choices

- **Access and deletion**: delete your account at any time in Settings.
- **Consent**: before any data is sent for AI processing, the app shows a dedicated
  disclosure naming what's sent and to whom; you must accept before your first message,
  document upload, voice recording, or project search is processed. You can review this
  disclosure again at any time in Settings → How your data is used.
- **Advertising choices**: on iOS, the system asks whether WakilAI may track you. If you
  choose *Ask App Not to Track*, we do not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA);
  you can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On
  Android, you can delete your advertising ID in your device settings (for example
  Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device); we
  then send an empty value instead. In both cases the Meta SDK may still send basic app
  events, such as install and app open, without your advertising ID. There is currently
  no separate in-app switch for advertising measurement.
- **Guest mode**: you can try the assistant without creating an account; the same
  consent and data-sharing terms apply to guest sessions.
- **Correction**: you can update your account details (name, email) via your sign-in
  provider at any time; changes sync on next sign-in.
- **Objection**: you can decline the AI-data disclosure at any point it's shown, which
  stops that action (sending a message, uploading a file, searching, or using voice)
  from proceeding.
- You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the applicable data protection
  authority in your jurisdiction.

## 9. Security

on-device storage for session credentials) to protect your data. No system is
completely secure; contact info@wakil.ai to report a suspected issue.
