# UTAX AI — Account and Data Deletion

This page applies to the UTAX AI app for Android and iOS, published under the app identifier `ai.humblebee.utax`.

## Request account deletion

Email [support@wakil.ai](mailto:support@wakil.ai?subject=UTAX%20AI%20account%20deletion) from the Google email address you use to sign in to UTAX AI. Use the subject **UTAX AI account deletion** and state that you want your UTAX AI account and its associated data deleted.

You do not need to install or sign in to the app to submit this request. Do not send your Google password, sign-in codes, or sensitive documents. We may ask for information needed to verify that the request belongs to you.

## Data covered by the request

Deletion covers your UTAX AI account/profile and identifiers, server-side chat history, messages, and uploaded attachments associated with the account. It does not delete your Google account or data held independently by Google.

The app may also store chats, attachment copies, and preferences locally on your device. Uninstall UTAX AI to remove its local app storage. Removing the app alone does not delete your server-side account or data.

## Retention

Account and associated service data are deleted after the request is verified and processed. Limited records may need to be retained to comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, or protect the service from abuse, as described in our privacy policy. If an exception applies to your request, support will explain the affected data, reason, and applicable retention period. No fixed deletion deadline is promised on this page.

## Delete chat or attachment data without deleting your account

You can also email support@wakil.ai to request deletion of specific server-side chats or attachments while keeping your account. Include the approximate chat date or other non-sensitive identifying information.

For more information, see the [UTAX AI Privacy Policy](https://github.com/wakil-ai/privayc-policies/blob/main/privacy-policy-utax-ai.md).
