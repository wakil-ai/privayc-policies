# Kusherbayev AI Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 23, 2026

This policy applies to Kusherbayev AI on iOS.

Kusherbayev AI ("we," "us," "our") is a legal assistant chat app built on the Wakil AI
CRM platform. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we
share it with, and the choices you have.

## 1. What the app does

Kusherbayev AI is a legal assistant chat app. You can send messages and optional
attachments, such as photos or files, to receive legal-assistant responses through the
app.

The app is not a substitute for advice from a licensed lawyer. Do not submit information
you are not comfortable sharing with the service provider.

## 2. Information we collect

The app may collect or process the following information:

- Chat messages you type into the app.
- Files, photos, or camera images you choose to attach to a chat message.
- Chat conversation identifiers needed to keep your conversations available in the app.
- Local app preferences, including language, theme, haptic feedback, and notification
  settings.
- Notification-related data needed to show "reply ready" notifications.
- Basic technical information that may be processed by the backend service when the app
  communicates with it, such as request metadata and server logs.

The app does not intentionally collect payment information, advertising identifiers,
precise location, contacts, health data, or biometric data.

## 3. Camera, photo library, and file access

The app requests camera access only when you choose to take a photo to attach to a chat
message.

The app requests photo library access only when you choose to attach an existing photo
to a chat message.

The app uses the system file picker only when you choose to attach a file. Files are not
accessed unless you select them.

## 4. How we use information

We use information to:

- Send your chat messages and attachments to the assistant service.
- Create and maintain chat conversations.
- Display previous conversations and attachments in the app.
- Notify you when a reply is ready, if notifications are enabled.
- Improve reliability, troubleshoot issues, and protect the service from abuse.
- Respond to support, privacy, or deletion requests.

## 5. Backend and third-party service providers

The app communicates with a Chatwoot-backed service hosted at:

`https://crm.wakil.ai`

Messages, attachments, and conversation identifiers may be processed by this backend
service so the app can provide chat functionality.

Service providers may process information only as needed to operate, secure, and
support the app.

## 6. Local storage on your device

The app stores some information locally on your device, including:

- Chat contact and conversation identifiers.
- Conversation list metadata, such as titles, previews, timestamps, and saved state.
- Local copies or references for attachments you send, so they can still display after
  the app restarts.
- App preferences, including language, theme, haptics, and notification settings.

Deleting the app from your device removes local app storage. It may not delete
information already sent to the backend service.

## 7. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app may send local "reply ready" notifications. You can
turn reply notifications on or off in the app settings, and you can also manage
notification permission in iOS Settings.

Notifications are not required to use the app.

## 8. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use your information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

We may share information only:

- With service providers needed to operate the chat service.
- When required by law, legal process, or to protect rights and safety.
- With your consent or at your direction.

## 9. Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the app, maintain conversation
history, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and operate the service.

Local data remains on your device until you delete a chat, delete the app, or use any
in-app deletion controls made available by the app.

Server-side chat and attachment data may remain in the backend service until deleted
under the service's retention and deletion process, including in response to a deletion
request as described below.

## 10. Your choices and deletion requests

You may:

- Choose not to attach files, photos, or camera images.
- Disable reply notifications in the app or iOS Settings.
- Delete individual chats from your device using the chat history controls described
  below.
- Delete the app to remove all local app data from your device.
- Request deletion of server-side chat/contact data using the deletion path below.

### Deleting a chat from your device

You can delete any conversation directly from the app's chat history: open the sessions
list and delete the chat you no longer want. This removes that conversation's messages,
local attachment copies, and history entry from this device immediately.

Deleting a chat this way removes it from your device only. It does not delete that
conversation from the backend service — the corresponding chat, attachment, contact, and
conversation data may still exist there until removed through the deletion request path
below.

### Requesting deletion of server-side data

The app does not currently include an in-app control that deletes data from the backend
service. To request deletion of server-side chat, attachment, contact, and conversation
data associated with your app-generated Chatwoot identity, contact:

support@wakil.ai

When you request deletion, include any information needed to identify your app
conversation, such as the approximate date of your chats or screenshots of the
conversation list. Do not include sensitive legal documents unless they are necessary to
identify the account or conversation.

We will confirm receipt of deletion requests and complete deletion within a reasonable
period, unless retention is required by law, dispute resolution, fraud prevention,
security, or another legitimate legal/business requirement.

## 11. Children

The app is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information
from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the
app, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

## 12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information
processed by the app and backend service. However, no method of transmission or storage
is completely secure.

## 13. International processing

Information may be processed in countries or regions where we or our service providers
operate. By using the app, you understand that information may be processed outside
your country or region.

## 14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we
will update the "Last updated" date and provide notice where required.
