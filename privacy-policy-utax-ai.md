# UTAX AI Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 6, 2026
**Effective date:** October 1, 2026

This policy applies to the UTAX AI mobile application on iOS and Android.

UTAX AI is an AI-powered tax and accounting information assistant operated by
HUMBLE BEE AI, MCHJ ("we," "us," and "our").
This policy explains what information the app processes, why it is used, and the
choices available to you.

## 1. What the app does

UTAX AI lets you send tax-related questions and optional attachments to receive
AI-assisted information. The app does not replace advice from a qualified tax,
legal, or financial professional. Do not submit information you are not
comfortable sharing with the service.

## 2. Information we collect or process

The app may collect or process:

- **Account information:** when you use Google Sign-In, your name, email address,
  Google account identifier, and profile image if provided by Google. When you use
  Telegram Sign-In, your Telegram account identifier, name, username, and profile
  image if provided by Telegram. We also process authentication tokens and a
  UTAX AI user/account identifier to verify your sign-in and associate your
  conversations with your account. UTAX AI does not receive your Google or
  Telegram password or sign-in codes.
- Chat messages and questions you submit.
- Photos, camera images, and files you choose to attach to a chat.
- **Financial information you choose to submit:** for example, income, assets,
  debts, or other tax/accounting information contained in your messages or
  uploaded documents. Providing these details is optional; use general questions
  or remove identifying details when possible.
- Conversation identifiers and message history needed to display and continue
  your chats.
- Local app preferences, including selected language and appearance mode.
- Basic technical and request information processed by the UTAX AI backend, such
  as app version, device type, request metadata, and error logs.
- **Diagnostics:** crash reports and technical error information collected by
  Sentry when error reporting is enabled, including stack traces and relevant
  app/device and request context. Diagnostic information may be associated with
  account or device context; it is not used for advertising tracking.

Account details, conversations, and attachments may be linked to your UTAX AI
account. Local appearance and language preferences are stored on your device.

Telegram Sign-In requests the `openid` and `profile` scopes. It does not request
your phone number or permission for the bot to message you, and does not give
UTAX AI access to your Telegram private chats or contacts.

UTAX AI does not intentionally collect payment card data, advertising identifiers,
precise location, contacts, health data, or biometric data.

## 3. Camera, photo library, and file access

The app requests access only when you choose the related action:

- **Camera:** to take a photo and attach it to a chat.
- **Photo library:** to select one or more existing images for a chat.
- **Files:** to select files through your device's system file picker.

UTAX AI does not access photos or files that you have not selected.

## 4. How we use information

We use information to:

- Authenticate users through Google or Telegram Sign-In and maintain their
  UTAX AI accounts.
- Send your question and selected attachments to the UTAX AI service.
- Create, maintain, and display your chat conversations.
- Provide tax-related AI-assisted responses.
- Store your local preferences and selected app language.
- Investigate errors, improve reliability, and protect the service from abuse.
- Respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.

We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising or
cross-app tracking.

## 5. Service providers and data sharing

UTAX AI communicates with systems and service providers needed to deliver the
chat service. Messages, attachments, and conversation identifiers may be processed
by those systems to provide, secure, maintain, and support the service.

- **Google** provides the Google Sign-In authentication service. Its independent
  processing is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Telegram** provides the Telegram Sign-In authentication service. Its
  independent processing is described in the [Telegram Privacy Policy](https://telegram.org/privacy).
- **AI and storage providers** process submitted messages, selected attachments,
  and relevant account/conversation identifiers on our behalf to provide the
  service. We do not authorize these providers to use UTAX AI user content to
  train their own models.
- **Sentry** processes crash and technical error reports on our behalf to help
  investigate failures and improve reliability when error reporting is enabled.

We may share information only with service providers operating the service, when
required by law or legal process, to protect rights and safety, or with your
consent or at your direction.

## 6. Local storage

The app may store information locally on your device, including chat metadata,
selected attachment references or copies, and app preferences. Deleting the app
removes its local storage from your device, but does not necessarily remove data
already sent to the backend service.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed to provide and operate the
service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service.

You can choose not to attach photos or files and can delete the app to remove its
local data. To request deletion of your UTAX AI account or associated server-side
chats, attachments, and conversation data, contact **support@utax.uz**. For Google
Sign-In accounts, use the associated email address where possible. For Telegram
Sign-In accounts, include your Telegram username or account identifier so support
can verify the request. You may also request deletion of specific conversations
while keeping your account. Include enough non-sensitive information to identify
the request, such as the approximate chat date. Do not send passwords,
authentication tokens, sign-in codes, or sensitive documents.

Submitting a request, signing out, or deleting the app does not itself delete
server-side data. In-app account deletion and end-to-end removal of associated
service data are not yet fully implemented. Contact support for the current status
of your request; this policy does not promise that complete deletion is currently
available or provide a fixed completion deadline. Deleting a UTAX AI account does
not delete your Google or Telegram account.

## 8. Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information
processed through UTAX AI. No method of transmission or storage is completely
secure.

Information may be processed in countries or regions where UTAX AI or its service
providers operate.

## 9. Children

UTAX AI is not intended for children. If you believe a child has provided personal
information through the app, contact us so we can review the request.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes,
we will update the "Last updated" date and provide notice where required.

## 11. Contact

For privacy, support, deletion requests, or general questions, contact
**support@utax.uz**.
